Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

The short version

Kube Console has no account, no server of its own and no analytics. It runs on your Mac and talks only to your Kubernetes clusters and your cloud providers, using the logins you already have.

What the app does with your data

  • Sign-in: handled by your cloud provider's own command-line tool (gcloud, aws or az) and their sign-in page. Kube Console never sees or stores your password.
  • Cluster data: app names, pod names, logs and details are read from your clusters and shown on your screen. The last list you saw for each cluster is kept on your Mac so it can appear instantly next time.
  • Settings: your choices, such as which clusters are production or read-only, are saved in a file on your Mac.
  • Your kubeconfig: when you first open a cluster, it may be added to your kubeconfig the same way your provider's tool would add it.

What the app doesn't do

  • It doesn't send usage data, crash reports or analytics anywhere.
  • It doesn't share anything with Kube Console's makers or anyone else.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies or analytics of its own. GitHub may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement. Requesting access opens a GitHub issue, which is public and subject to GitHub's terms.
